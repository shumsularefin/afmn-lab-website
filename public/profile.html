Biography

Md. Kamruzzaman is Professor of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at Gopalganj Science and Technology University (GSTU), Bangladesh, and Chairman of the department.

Born in Chapai Nawabganj, Bangladesh, he received his B.Sc. (Honours) in 2002 and M.Sc. in 2003 in Applied Chemistry and Chemical Technology from the University of Dhaka, both in the first class. He then joined the Environmental Microbiology Laboratory of icddr,b as a research assistant, working on projects funded by Dartmouth College, the World Bank and UNICEF.

In 2010 he moved to the Spectroanalytical Chemistry Laboratory at Kyungpook National University, Daegu, Republic of Korea, where he received his Ph.D. in Analytical Chemistry in February 2013. His doctoral work introduced a series of nanoparticle-enhanced lanthanide luminescence and microfluidic chemiluminescence methods, published in Food Chemistry, Sensors and Actuators B, Microchimica Acta, Biomedical Microdevices and the Journal of Luminescence.

After teaching at Daffodil International University and American International University–Bangladesh, he joined GSTU (then Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Science and Technology University) in April 2018. He was promoted to Associate Professor in November 2021 and to Professor on 1 July 2026. At GSTU he is Principal Investigator of the Advanced Functional Materials and Nanotechnology (AFMN) Laboratory, whose work on MXene–biopolymer nanocomposites has appeared in ACS Applied Engineering Materials, RSC Sustainability, Packaging Technology and Science and Materials Chemistry and Physics.

Teaching and service

He chairs the Department of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at GSTU, teaches Inorganic Chemistry, Chemical Technology, Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Technology, and Textile Engineering, and supervises B.Sc. and M.Sc. thesis research. He is Faculty Advisor of both the ACS and AIChE student chapters at GSTU. See Teaching.

Student chapters at GSTU

Prof. Kamruzzaman is Faculty Advisor of two professional student chapters at Gopalganj Science and Technology University.

American Chemical Society (ACS) Student Chapter

Prof. Kamruzzaman is the founding Faculty Advisor of the American Chemical Society Student Chapter, GSTU, established in 2024 together with Co-Advisor S. M. Fazle Rabbi. The chapter runs outreach, workshops, career events and sustainability campaigns for students across the university. Under his advisorship it has organised outreach webinars, public sustainability campaigns and career events, and has been recognised by the American Chemical Society.
