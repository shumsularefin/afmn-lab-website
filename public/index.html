AFMN Lab

The Advanced Functional Materials and Nanotechnology (AFMN) Laboratory at Gopalganj Science and Technology University designs MXene, carbon-nitride and graphene nanocomposites in biodegradable polymers for UV protection, food packaging and clean water, and develops nanoparticle-enhanced luminescence methods for measuring molecules at trace levels.

Research

Five connected programmes in the Advanced Functional Materials and Nanotechnology (AFMN) Laboratory, from nanosheet synthesis to applied analysis. All research

01

UV and blue-light shielding films

Transparent MXene and g-C3N4 films in chitosan, alginate and PMMA that absorb UV and high-energy blue light.

 02

Biodegradable packaging and biomaterials

Antibacterial, antioxidant alginate/MXene packaging films, keratin bioplastics and wound patches.

 03

Luminescence sensing

Lanthanide and nanoparticle-enhanced fluorescence methods for drugs, bilirubin and proteins in serum.

 04

Microfluidic chemiluminescence

Chip-based, nanoparticle-catalysed chemiluminescence for vitamins and amino acids in food and medicines.

 05

Water treatment

Graphene, lignin and alginate adsorbents for dyes, Cr(VI) and lead in textile effluent.

Student chapters at GSTU
